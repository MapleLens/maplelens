Connect Discord

MapleLens posts each sale to a channel through a webhook: a private link to that one channel. You need the Manage Webhooks permission there (your own server always works).

  1. Open the channel's settingsHover the channel that should get the messages and click its gear (Edit Channel).
  2. Make a webhookIntegrations → Webhooks → New Webhook. Name it and give it a picture if you like.
  3. Copy its URLClick Copy Webhook URL. Keep it private: whoever has it can post in that channel.
  4. Paste it hereNext to Discord, click Add, paste, press Enter, then Test. A sample sale shows up in the channel.
Connect Telegram

MapleLens writes through a bot of your own, which only you control. It takes about a minute.

  1. Make a botIn Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, pick a name and a username ending in “bot”. It answers with the bot's token: copy it.
  2. Say hello to itOpen your new bot (BotFather links it) and press Start. For a group, add the bot to the group and send any message there.
  3. Paste the token hereNext to Telegram, click Add, paste the token, press Enter, then Test. MapleLens finds your chat, sends a sample sale and builds your webhook URL.

Already have one? Paste the whole URL instead: https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/sendMessage?chat_id=<chat id>. Keep the token private, and if it ever leaks, send /revoke to @BotFather for a new one.
Connect your phone
  1. Point your phone's camera at this codeAny camera app that reads QR codes works.
  2. Open the link and turn on notificationsTap Turn on notifications and allow them.
  3. On iPhone, add it to the home screen firstShare, then Add to Home Screen, and open MapleLens from there. The page shows how.
